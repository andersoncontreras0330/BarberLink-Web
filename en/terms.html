The rules for using BarberLink, as a client or as a barber.

1. What BarberLink is

An app that connects barbers and clients: profiles, bookings, reminders and ratings.

BarberLink does not provide barbering services. Each barber and barbershop is responsible for their services, prices, hours and treatment.

2. Your account

You must give true information and keep your password safe. What is done with your account is your responsibility.

Each account is a client or a barber account, and that does not change. If you are a barber and also want to book as a client, use another email.

You must be at least 13. If you are a minor in your country, you need your parents' or guardians' permission.

3. Bookings

Booking reserves a time with the barber. Home visits and fixed slots stay pending until the barber accepts them.

The price you see is set by the barber, and includes the seasonal increase or the home travel fee when they apply.

The service is paid directly to the barber or barbershop. BarberLink does not charge for services or process those payments.

You can cancel or move your appointment in the app. Please do it at least 2 hours ahead so the barber can offer that time. BarberLink charges no penalties; any policy of a barber's own is an agreement between you.

The barber can cancel an appointment too. If they do, you get a notice.

4. If you are a barber

To show up and take bookings, each barber needs an active subscription: monthly or yearly, with the first month free. Prices are those shown in the app and the App Store.

Apple charges the subscription to your App Store account and it renews automatically unless you cancel it at least 24 hours before the period ends. You manage and cancel it in Settings › your name › Subscriptions.

Without an active subscription your profile stops showing to new clients, and your data is kept.

You are responsible for your services, prices, hours and photos being true, and for having the right to use the photos you upload.

A barbershop owner answers for the barbers they invite to the team and for the barbershop's information.

5. Ratings

Only someone who went to the appointment can rate it, once. Ratings are anonymous and are shown once there are at least 5.

Rating from fake accounts or trying to manipulate ratings is not allowed.

6. Acceptable use

Do not use BarberLink for anything illegal, to impersonate someone, to post offensive content or content you have no right to use, or to try to access data that is not yours.

We may suspend or close accounts that do not follow these terms.

7. Liability

We offer BarberLink as is. We work to keep it running, but we do not guarantee it will be free of interruptions or errors.

To the extent the law allows, BarberLink is not liable for the services a barber provides or for disagreements between barbers and clients.

8. Deleting your account

You can delete it whenever you want from the app. "Delete your account" explains what is deleted.

9. Changes, governing law and contact

If we change these terms we will let you know in the app.
