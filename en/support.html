Support

Need help? Write to barberlink.soporte@gmail.com. We answer within one or two business days.

How do I find my barber?

With their code or QR (on Home, "Find by code or QR"), or on the map in Explore.

How do I cancel or move an appointment?

In Bookings, tap the appointment and choose Reschedule or Cancel. Please do it at least 2 hours ahead.

I am not getting notifications

Check that they are allowed in your phone's Settings › BarberLink › Notifications. In the app, clients turn them on in Profile › Appointment reminders and barbers in More › Notifications.

How do I cancel my barber subscription?

On the iPhone, in Settings › your name › Subscriptions › BarberLink.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Profile › Delete my account (clients) or More › Delete my account (barbers). More details in "Delete your account".
