Privacy Policy

We keep what is needed for you to book and run appointments. We do not sell data. Everyone sees only their own.

1. What we collect

When you sign up: your name, email, password, whether you are a barber or a client, and the language you choose.

If you are a client: your phone number, only if you choose to add it; the addresses you save for home visits, with their spot on the map; your favorite barbershops and saved barbers.

If you are a barber: your name, specialty and photo; your services with their prices, durations and example photos; your hours, breaks, blocked times and days off; and how you work at clients' homes (distance and fee). If you have a barbershop: its name, phone, address and spot on the map, its hours, its cover and gallery, and the name, email, phone and specialty of the barbers you invite to your team.

Appointments: with whom, which services, date and time, price, status, whether you asked for a reminder or a fixed slot and, for home visits, the address and distance.

A barber's notes on their clients: whether they marked them as VIP or favorite, and what they write down.

Ratings: stars and tags from a fixed list. There are no text comments.

Location: only if you allow it, and only while you use the app, to show nearby barbershops and distances, or to put your barbershop or your address on the map. We do not track it in the background, and it is only saved when you use it for an address or for your barbershop.

Camera and photos: only when you take or pick a photo, or scan a barber's QR code. Only the photo you choose to upload is saved.

From your phone: the identifier Apple or Google gives it to receive notifications, and whether it is an iPhone or an Android.

We do not collect data for advertising and we do not use third-party trackers.

2. What we use it for

So you can find your barber and book their free times, and so the barber can run their schedule.

To notify you about confirmations, cancellations, changes and reminders of your appointments.

To show the rating summary of each barber and barbershop.

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell it or share it with third parties for commercial purposes.

3. Who sees what

Anyone with the app sees the profile of published barbers and barbershops: name, photo, specialty, services and prices, hours, address and location, photos, code and ratings.

Your barber sees your name, your phone number if you added it, and your appointments with them. For a home visit they first see only the area; the exact address once they accept the visit.

A barbershop owner sees the barbers in their team and can set up their services and hours.

Ratings are anonymous: nobody sees who rated or any single rating. Only the average is shown, once there are at least 5.

Nobody else sees your appointments, your addresses or the notes about you. This is enforced by the database rules, not the app: they reject any query for someone else's data.

4. Who it is shared with

Supabase, which hosts the database and photos on our behalf and under our instructions.

Expo, which delivers notifications to your phone through Apple or Google: it receives your phone's notification identifier and the text of the notice.

If you are a barber and subscribe: Apple, which charges the subscription, and RevenueCat, which confirms its status to us. They receive your user identifier and the purchase details; not your appointments or clients.

When you tap "Get directions", the address opens in the maps app you pick (Google Maps, Waze or Apple Maps).

An authority, when a valid legal order requires it.

Nobody else.

5. How long we keep it

While your account exists. The bell's notifications are kept for 90 days.

If you delete your account, your upcoming appointments are cancelled and the other person is notified. Your personal data, photos, addresses, favorites, the notes by and about you, your email and your login are deleted.

If you had appointments, the other person keeps that history with your name marked "(cuenta eliminada)", because it is their record too. Ratings stay, because they are anonymous.

The owner of a barbershop with barbers in the team has to remove them before deleting the account, so nobody loses their barbershop by surprise.

6. Your rights

Access and correct your data: you can edit it yourself in the app.

Delete your account: in the app, Profile › Delete my account (clients) or More › Delete my account (barbers), or by writing to us.

Ask for a copy of your data or object to a use of it, by writing to barberlink.soporte@gmail.com

7. Security

All traffic between the app and the server is encrypted.

Access to the data is controlled by the database row by row, not by the app: even someone querying the server outside the app would only get their own.

Passwords are stored encrypted and nobody, including us, can read them.

Your appointment reminders are scheduled on your phone. When you log out they are removed, and the phone stops getting your notifications.

8. Children

BarberLink is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If we find an account of a child under that age, we close it and delete its data.

9. Changes and contact

If we change this policy we will let you know in the app.

Data controller and contact: barberlink.soporte@gmail.com
