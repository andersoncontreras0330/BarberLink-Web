Delete your account

You can delete your BarberLink account at any time, from the app.

From the app

Clients: Profile › Delete my account.

Barbers: More › Delete my account.

The app shows you what will happen before you confirm.

What is deleted

Your login, your email and your personal data: your phone, photos, addresses, favorites, saved barbers and the notes by and about you.

Your upcoming appointments are cancelled and the other person is notified.

If you are a barber, you stop showing up for clients; if you have a barbershop, it does too, with its photos.

What is kept

If you had appointments, the other person keeps that history with your name marked "(cuenta eliminada)".

Ratings, which are anonymous.

Before deleting it

If you own a barbershop with barbers in your team, remove them first in More › My barbershop & team.

Deleting your account does not cancel your App Store subscription. Cancel it in Settings › your name › Subscriptions.

Without access to the app

Write to barberlink.soporte@gmail.com from your account's email with the subject "Delete my account". We delete it within 30 days.
